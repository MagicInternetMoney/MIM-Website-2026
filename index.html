Lore and narrative

The joke was early. The spell was real.

MIMCOIN™ was the original Magic Internet Money™, launched in the 2013 and 2014 forum era as a playful altcoin experiment by Christopher Franko, long before memecoins became a market category.

The name was a wink at early Bitcoin culture, including the forum-era line “magic internet money, bro” - and MIMCOIN™ turned that wink into a brand.

It was mineable, whimsical, and deliberately unserious in a scene that often tried very hard to look serious. The original MIMCOIN™ is the lineage this Solana token continues: community, code, humor, and culture coordinating in public.
